Privacy Policy for Bella’s Playhouse

Effective 23 September 2026 · Last updated 23 September 2026

Bella’s Playhouse is a collection of twelve mini-games made for young children. It has no accounts, no sign-in, no chat, and no purchases. We do not collect personal information from anyone who plays it.

This policy explains, in plain language, what the app does and does not do with information. It applies to the Bella’s Playhouse Android app published by Valter Klug LLC under the developer name Klugames.

What we collect

Nothing. The app never asks for a name, an email address, a phone number, a birthday or a photo. It does not use the camera, the microphone, the contacts list, the photo library or GPS. There is no registration, no login and no profile.

What stays on the device

The app saves a small amount of information on the phone or tablet itself so the games behave the way a child expects:

  • High scores and best times for each game
  • Whether background music and sound effects are switched on
  • Which board size was last chosen in the games that offer one

This is stored in the app’s own private storage on the device. It is never sent to us, never sent to anyone else, and cannot be read by other apps. Deleting the app removes it permanently.

Advertising

The app shows a single banner advertisement at the top of the screen. Ads are served by Google AdMob, and they are the only reason the app connects to the internet at all. Everything else works offline.

Because this is an app for children, we have configured AdMob with the strictest settings it offers:

  • Child-directed treatment is switched on, so Google treats every request as coming from a child under the applicable age, as required by COPPA.
  • Users are treated as below the age of consent under European rules.
  • Only non-personalized ads are requested. Ads are not selected based on any profile of the person playing.
  • The maximum ad content rating is set to G, so only advertising suitable for general audiences, including families and children, can appear.

Even with personalization off, Google still processes limited technical information in order to deliver an advertisement, guard against fraud and stop the same ad repeating endlessly. This typically includes the device model and operating system version, the approximate region derived from the network address, and a non-personalized identifier used for that purpose. We never receive this information, and it is not linked by us to any individual.

How Google handles that data is described in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services.

What the app does not do

  • No analytics or usage-tracking software of any kind
  • No social network logins, share buttons or third-party sign-in
  • No in-app purchases, subscriptions or payments
  • No links out to websites or other apps from inside the games
  • No advertising other than the single banner described above
  • No selling or sharing of information, because there is none to sell or share

Children

This app is designed for children and is published in the Google Play family programme. We comply with the Children’s Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and with Google Play’s Families policy.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children, or from anyone else. Since no personal information is collected, there is no account to close and no data for a parent to request or delete. Removing the app from the device removes everything the app has saved.

If you are a parent or guardian with a question or concern about this app, please write to us at the address below and we will answer.

Permissions the app requests

The app requests only what the advertisement requires:

  • Internet access, to load the banner ad
  • Advertising identifier, used by Google for the purposes described above

It requests no other permission of any kind.

Changes to this policy

If the app changes in a way that affects this policy, we will update this page and change the date at the top. Material changes will also be reflected in the app’s Google Play listing.
